Market data

Completed transactions are preferred over asking prices. Transactions are normalized and deduplicated before summary metrics are calculated.

Card identity

Sport, player, year, set, card number, variant, serial number and grade are treated as separate dimensions when available.

Model discipline

Observed prices are separated from modeled grading scenarios. Low-volume cards receive additional caution because a single sale can distort a short window.

Reference framework

The universe follows established hobby categories including baseball, basketball, football, hockey, soccer and boxing/MMA, with additional categories for golf, racing, tennis and wrestling. PSA's public category structure also separates these sports and collectible types.

This dashboard is a research project. Market values can change quickly and observed transaction prices are historical observations, not guarantees.
