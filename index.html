Raw → PSA 10 price spread

The spread is a screening metric. It does not account for grading cost, selling fees, shipping, taxes, grade probability or time-to-sale.

Data rules

Completed sales are preferred to asking prices. Base cards, refractors, autos and serial-numbered parallels are kept separate rather than blended into one series.

Market logic

Price, scarcity, liquidity and grade spread are evaluated together. Low-volume cards receive more cautious interpretation because one transaction can move an apparent market level.

Model discipline

Scenario values are labeled as scenarios. No future price is treated as guaranteed, and grade outcomes are modeled separately from observed market prices.
